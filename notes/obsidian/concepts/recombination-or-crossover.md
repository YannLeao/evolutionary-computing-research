# recombination

## Definition
As the names indicate, such an operator merges information from two parent genotypes into one or two offspring genotypes. Like mutation, recombination is a stochastic operator

The principle behind recombination is simple – by mating two individuals with different but desirable features, we can produce an offspring that combines both of those features.
