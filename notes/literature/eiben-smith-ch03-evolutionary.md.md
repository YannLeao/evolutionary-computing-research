##  Main Ideas

### Active forces
There are two main forces that drive evolutionary algorithms:  
  
1. **Variation operators** ([[mutation]] and [[recombination]]) introduce diversity into the population.  
2. **Selection** acts as a pressure that increases the average fitness of the population.

### Dialects diferences
Different evolutionary computing paradigms (or "dialects") are mainly distinguished by how candidate solutions are represented.  
  
This representation affects how variation operators ([[mutation]] and [[recombination]]) work, but it does **not fundamentally change the role of selection**.
